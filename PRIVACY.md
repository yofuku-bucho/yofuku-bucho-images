# Privacy Policy — Yofuku Bucho Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-23

"Yofuku Bucho Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "洋服部長 (Yofuku Bucho)" (https://www.youtube.com/@yofuku_bucho).

- **What it does:** it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos created by the channel owner to the owner's own channel, and to set their titles, descriptions and publish times.
- **Data accessed:** only the owner's own YouTube channel, through Google OAuth. No other user's account or data is accessed.
- **Data collected:** none. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.
- **Storage:** the OAuth access token for the owner's channel is kept privately on the owner's server and used only for the uploads above. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- **YouTube Terms:** use of this tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Contact: through the YouTube channel above.
